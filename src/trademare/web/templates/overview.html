New here? How TradeMare works

  1. Every evening, after each market closes, the system downloads prices and company data and ranks the stocks.
  2. It proposes trades as signals: what to buy or sell, how many shares, and a stop-loss{{ tip("stop", "stop-loss") }}, the price at which it would sell to cap the loss.
  3. You decide. Open Signals, read "Explain this signal", then approve or reject. Nothing is bought without you.
  4. Approved trades fill at the next market open and appear in your Trade journal with their profit or loss.
  5. Until a strategy is proven on history it was never tuned on, everything is paper trading: real prices, pretend money.

Look for the i icons: hover or tap one to see what a term means.
